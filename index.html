One-to-one or small group

Private yoga, shaped around you.

Personal lessons can suit beginners wanting to build confidence, people who prefer a quieter setting, or anyone hoping to focus on a particular posture or practice. Sessions are available at Maureen's home studio or in your home.

At the home studio: £40 for one hour, or four sessions for £140. At your home: £45 for one hour, or four sessions for £160.

A quiet, fully clothed treatment

Make space to rest.

Reiki is a Japanese complementary practice often chosen as a way to relax. During a session, you lie fully clothed on a treatment couch while Maureen uses a light touch or holds her hands just above the body. You can talk through comfort and preferences beforehand.

Single treatment £40 · Course of three £105. Reiki is complementary and is not a substitute for medical care.

Ask Maureen about Reiki ↗

A one-to-one sound session

Tibetan singing bowl therapy.

Settle fully clothed on a mattress on the floor while Maureen plays Tibetan singing bowls. The session is an hour, with time to get comfortable before the sounds begin.

One-hour treatment · £50. Let Maureen know about any comfort or access needs when arranging your visit.

Ask Maureen about a treatment ↗

A shared pause

Let the sound carry you away.

Begin with a guided chakra meditation; no meditation experience is needed. Then lie on a mat or sit comfortably while Maureen plays gongs, Tibetan singing bowls, gentle drums and other instruments.

Bring a mat, blanket and cushions so you can settle in warmly. The hall floor can feel cold; a limited number of mats may be available to borrow. Wear comfortable clothes.

Monthly at Baginton Village Hall, 6–7pm · £15 in advance or £18 on the day. Pre-booking is essential.

A little about your teacher

Meet Maureen.

Maureen teaches Sun Power Yoga and is a Level 3 member of the Register of Exercise Professionals. She began teaching to share the health and happiness she found through a regular yoga practice.

Her classes bring together people from all walks of life. Whether you're new to yoga or returning to your practice, you'll be encouraged to listen to your own body and find your own way through each session.

Come and say hello ↗

New to yoga?

Your first class, made easy.

No special kit or experience needed. The most important thing is to let Maureen know if you have an injury or health concern before class begins.

01

Wear what moves

Comfortable clothes such as leggings, loose trousers or a T-shirt. Yoga is practised in bare feet.

02

Everything else is here

Mats are available, along with blocks, belts and blankets. Bring your own mat if you prefer.

03

Take it at your pace

All ages, body types and experience levels are welcome. There is no need to keep up or compete.
