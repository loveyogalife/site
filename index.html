A little about your teacher

Meet Maureen.

Maureen teaches Sun Power Yoga and is a Level 3 member of the Register of Exercise Professionals. She began teaching to share the health and happiness she found through a regular yoga practice.

Her classes bring together people from all walks of life. Whether you're new to yoga or returning to your practice, you'll be encouraged to listen to your own body and find your own way through each session.

Come and say hello ↗

New to yoga?

Your first class, made easy.

No special kit or experience needed. The most important thing is to let Maureen know if you have an injury or health concern before class begins.

01

Wear what moves

Comfortable clothes such as leggings, loose trousers or a T-shirt. Yoga is practised in bare feet.

02

Everything else is here

Mats are available, along with blocks, belts and blankets. Bring your own mat if you prefer.

03

Take it at your pace

All ages, body types and experience levels are welcome. There is no need to keep up or compete.
